# Searches that find competitors

Search the way a buyer would, not the way the owner describes themselves. Replace `<service>`
with the buyer's words for it (try two or three ways of saying it), `<who>` with the buyer, and
`<town>` with the area for a local business. Run 15 to 25 of these; stop early when new searches
stop turning up new names.

## Online sellers (coaches, agencies, courses, memberships, software)

- `<service> for <who>`
- `<service> for <who> pricing`
- `<service> program price`
- `<service> packages`
- `<service> monthly retainer` (agencies) or `<service> per month`
- `<service> course` and `<service> online course price`
- `<service> membership` and `<service> community` (finds Skool, Circle and similar groups)
- `<service> mastermind price`
- `best <service> for <who>` (for names only, never prices)
- `<a competitor you already found> alternative` and `<that competitor> vs`
- `<service> money back guarantee`
- `<service> done for you`
- `hire <service> agency` / `<service> consultant rates`

## Local sellers

- `<service> <town>`
- `<service> near <town>` and `<service> <neighbourhood>`
- `<service> <town> price` and `<service> cost <town>`
- `best <service> <town>` (for names only)
- `<service> <town> reviews`

## What to keep

A real business that sells the same service to the same buyer and has its own page. Not a
listing site, a review site, a marketplace category page, a news article or a "how much does it
cost" guide. A cost guide may still be read for context, but its prices are not a competitor's
prices and never go in the table.
